    How it works

    What happens between choosing an X-ray and reading the report.

    1. Your browser reads the X-ray

      A chest X-ray model (Microsoft's RAD-DINO, compressed from 330 MB to 86 MB with no loss in accuracy) turns the image into 768 numbers in about half a second. For search, the image never leaves your device.

    2. Search the case library

      Those numbers are compared with 2,517 reported X-rays in about 2 ms. The five closest come back with their diagnoses and reports.

    3. Draft with evidence

      Gemini sees the X-ray, the five reports and a checklist of findings to look for. Every sentence must cite its source: this X-ray, or a similar case that shares the finding.

    4. Second look

      A second pass goes through the checklist again, looking only for findings the draft missed, and adds them.

    5. Check every sentence

      Each sentence is checked against the image and the cases it cites. Unsupported sentences are revised, or removed and shown separately.

    6. Trace and measure

      Every step, prompt version and evaluation score is recorded in Langfuse, so any report can be traced back to exactly what produced it.

    Built with Python, FastAPI, LangGraph, Gemini, Langfuse, ONNX Runtime Web and PyTorch. 81 automated tests.
